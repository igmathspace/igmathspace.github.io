🎯 1. Learning Outcomes (CO2)
  • State and prove the First Shifting Theorem (s-Shifting)
  • Apply the theorem to find Laplace transforms of \( e^{at}f(t) \)
  • Use the theorem to find inverse Laplace transforms by completing the square
  • Solve problems involving damped vibrations and shifted transforms
📌 2. Recall: Standard Laplace Transform Pairs
  • \(1\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{1}{s}\)\(s>0\)
  • \(t^n\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{n!}{s^{n+1}}\)\(s>0\)
  • \(\cos \omega t\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{s}{s^2+\omega^2}\)\(s>0\)
  • \(\sin \omega t\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{\omega}{s^2+\omega^2}\)\(s>0\)
📐 3. Theorem: First Shifting Theorem (s-Shifting)
Theorem 2 (First Shifting Theorem)

If \( f(t) \) has the transform \( F(s) \) (where \( s > k \) for some \( k \)), then \( e^{at}f(t) \) has the transform \( F(s - a) \) (where \( s - a > k \)). In formulas:

\[ \boxed{ \mathcal{L}\{e^{at}f(t)\} = F(s - a) } \]

Equivalently, for the inverse transform:

\[ \boxed{ e^{at}f(t) = \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s - a)\} } \]
📝 4. Proof of the First Shifting Theorem

We obtain \( F(s - a) \) by replacing \( s \) with \( s - a \) in the defining integral:

\(F(s - a) = \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-(s-a)t} f(t) \, dt\)
\(= \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} \bigl[ e^{at} f(t) \bigr] \, dt\)
\(= \mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} f(t) \}.\)

✅ This proves the theorem. Taking the inverse on both sides gives the second formula.

⚠️ CAUTION! \(-a\) appears in \(F(s - a)\) but \(+a\) appears in \(e^{at}f(t)\).
📊 6. Important Formulas – Damped Vibrations

From the first shifting theorem, we get:

\(\mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \cos \omega t \} = \dfrac{s - a}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \sin \omega t \} = \dfrac{\omega}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2}\)

These are used to solve damped vibration problems.

📚 7. Workout Examples

1. Find \( \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{12}{(s-3)^4} \right\} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{12}{(s-3)^4} \right\} = 12 \cdot \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{1}{(s-3)^4} \right\}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{t^3\} = \dfrac{3!}{s^4} = \dfrac{6}{s^4}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{t^3 e^{3t}\} = \dfrac{6}{(s-3)^4}\)
\(\therefore \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{12}{(s-3)^4} \right\} = \dfrac{12}{6} \cdot t^3 e^{3t} = 2t^3 e^{3t}\)

✅ \( f(t) = 2t^3 e^{3t} \)

2. Find \( \mathcal{L}\{ t^2 e^{at} \} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(\mathcal{L}\{t^2\} = \dfrac{2!}{s^3} = \dfrac{2}{s^3}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{ t^2 e^{at} \} = \left[ \mathcal{L}\{t^2\} \right]_{s \to s-a} = \dfrac{2}{(s-a)^3}\)

✅ \( \mathcal{L}\{ t^2 e^{at} \} = \dfrac{2}{(s-a)^3} \)

3. Find \( \mathcal{L}\{ \sinh t \cos t \} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(\sinh t \cos t = \dfrac{e^{t} - e^{-t}}{2} \cdot \cos t = \dfrac{1}{2} e^{t} \cos t - \dfrac{1}{2} e^{-t} \cos t\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \cos \omega t \} = \dfrac{s - a}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{ \sinh t \cos t \} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left[ \dfrac{s - 1}{(s - 1)^2 + 1} - \dfrac{s + 1}{(s + 1)^2 + 1} \right]\)

✅ \( \mathcal{L}\{ \sinh t \cos t \} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left[ \dfrac{s - 1}{(s - 1)^2 + 1} - \dfrac{s + 1}{(s + 1)^2 + 1} \right] \)

4. Find \( \mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \cos \omega t \} \) and \( \mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \sin \omega t \} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(\mathcal{L}\{\cos \omega t\} = \dfrac{s}{s^2 + \omega^2}, \quad \mathcal{L}\{\sin \omega t\} = \dfrac{\omega}{s^2 + \omega^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \cos \omega t \} = \left[ \dfrac{s}{s^2 + \omega^2} \right]_{s \to s-a} = \dfrac{s - a}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \sin \omega t \} = \left[ \dfrac{\omega}{s^2 + \omega^2} \right]_{s \to s-a} = \dfrac{\omega}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2}\)

✅ \( \mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \cos \omega t \} = \dfrac{s - a}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2} \), \( \mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \sin \omega t \} = \dfrac{\omega}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2} \)

5. Find \( \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{-s+11}{s^2 - 2s - 3} \right\} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(s^2 - 2s - 3 = (s-1)^2 - 4 = (s-1)^2 - 2^2\)
\(\dfrac{-s+11}{(s-1)^2 - 4} = \dfrac{-(s-1) + 10}{(s-1)^2 - 4}\)
\(= -\dfrac{s-1}{(s-1)^2 - 4} + 10 \cdot \dfrac{1}{(s-1)^2 - 4}\)
\(= -\dfrac{s-1}{(s-1)^2 - 4} + \dfrac{10}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{(s-1)^2 - 4}\)
\(= -\dfrac{s-1}{(s-1)^2 - 4} + 5 \cdot \dfrac{2}{(s-1)^2 - 4}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1} = -e^{t} \cosh(2t) + 5 e^{t} \sinh(2t)\)

✅ \( f(t) = e^{t}(5\sinh(2t) - \cosh(2t)) \)

6. If \( \mathcal{L}[f(t)] = \dfrac{3s-137}{s^2+2s+401} \), find \( f(t) \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step (Completing the square):
\(s^2+2s+401 = (s+1)^2 + 400 = (s+1)^2 + 20^2\)
\(\dfrac{3s-137}{(s+1)^2+20^2} = \dfrac{3(s+1) - 140}{(s+1)^2+20^2}\)
\(= 3 \cdot \dfrac{s+1}{(s+1)^2+20^2} - \dfrac{140}{20} \cdot \dfrac{20}{(s+1)^2+20^2}\)
\(= 3 \cdot \dfrac{s+1}{(s+1)^2+20^2} - 7 \cdot \dfrac{20}{(s+1)^2+20^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1} = 3 e^{-t} \cos(20t) - 7 e^{-t} \sin(20t)\)

✅ \( f(t) = e^{-t}(3\cos 20t - 7\sin 20t) \)

7. Find \( \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{6s+7}{2s^2+4s+10} \right\} \) and \( \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{a}{(s+1)^2} \right\} \) CO2
✅ Part 1:
\(\dfrac{6s+7}{2s^2+4s+10} = \dfrac{6s+7}{2(s^2+2s+5)} = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{6s+7}{s^2+2s+5}\)
\(s^2+2s+5 = (s+1)^2 + 4 = (s+1)^2 + 2^2\)
\(\dfrac{6s+7}{(s+1)^2+4} = \dfrac{6(s+1) + 1}{(s+1)^2+4}\)
\(= 6 \cdot \dfrac{s+1}{(s+1)^2+4} + \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{(s+1)^2+4}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left[ 6 e^{-t} \cos(2t) + \dfrac{1}{2} e^{-t} \sin(2t) \right]\)
\(= 3 e^{-t} \cos(2t) + \dfrac{1}{4} e^{-t} \sin(2t)\)
✅ Part 2:
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{a}{(s+1)^2} \right\} = a \cdot \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{1}{(s+1)^2} \right\}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{t\} = \dfrac{1}{s^2} \Rightarrow \mathcal{L}\{t e^{-t}\} = \dfrac{1}{(s+1)^2}\)
\(\therefore \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left\{ \dfrac{a}{(s+1)^2} \right\} = a t e^{-t}\)
8. Find Laplace of (a) \( \cos^2 \omega t \) (b) \( \cosh 2t - 1 \) (c) \( e^{at} \cos \omega t \) CO2
✅ (a) \( \cos^2 \omega t \):
\(\cos^2 \omega t = \dfrac{1 + \cos 2\omega t}{2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{\cos^2 \omega t\} = \dfrac{1}{2}\mathcal{L}\{1\} + \dfrac{1}{2}\mathcal{L}\{\cos 2\omega t\}\)
\(= \dfrac{1}{2s} + \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 + (2\omega)^2} = \dfrac{1}{2s} + \dfrac{s}{2(s^2 + 4\omega^2)}\)
✅ (b) \( \cosh 2t - 1 \):
\(\mathcal{L}\{\cosh 2t - 1\} = \mathcal{L}\{\cosh 2t\} - \mathcal{L}\{1\}\)
\(= \dfrac{s}{s^2 - 4} - \dfrac{1}{s}\)
✅ (c) \( e^{at} \cos \omega t \):
\(\mathcal{L}\{ e^{at} \cos \omega t \} = \left[ \mathcal{L}\{\cos \omega t\} \right]_{s \to s-a}\)
\(= \dfrac{s - a}{(s - a)^2 + \omega^2}\)
📋 8. Quick Summary
  • First Shifting Theorem: \(\mathcal{L}\{e^{at}f(t)\} = F(s-a)\)
  • Inverse Form: \(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s-a)\} = e^{at}f(t)\)
  • Damped Vibrations: \(\mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\cos\omega t\} = \dfrac{s-a}{(s-a)^2+\omega^2}\), \(\mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\sin\omega t\} = \dfrac{\omega}{(s-a)^2+\omega^2}\)
  • Key Technique: Complete the square to identify shifted forms.
