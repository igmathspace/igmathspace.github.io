STATUS: OUT OF SYLLABUS

The topic listed above for Lecture 50 is not part of the prescribed syllabus for the course KU3DSCMAT211 – Differential Equations, Laplace Transforms, Linear Programming and Numerical Methods.

The Module IV syllabus (Page 2, Course Contents) explicitly lists only the following for the unit "Solution of algebraic and transcendental equations":

  • a) Introduction (covered in Lecture 46)
  • b) Bisection Method (covered in Lectures 47 & 48)
  • c) Newton-Raphson Method (to be covered in Lectures 51–53)

The False Position (Regula Falsi) Method and its convergence comparison with the Bisection method are excluded from the official course contents and will not appear in the end-semester examination. Sastry's §2.3 is not listed in the Reference Distribution table on Page 3.
