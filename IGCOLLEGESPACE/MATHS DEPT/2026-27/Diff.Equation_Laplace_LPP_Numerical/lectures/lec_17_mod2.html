🎯 1. Learning Outcomes (CO2)
  • State and prove the linearity property of the Laplace transform
  • Apply linearity to find transforms of combinations of functions
  • State and apply the linearity property of the inverse Laplace transform
  • Use linearity to find inverse transforms of rational functions
📌 2. Recall: Standard Laplace Transform Pairs
  • \(1\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{1}{s}\)\(s>0\)
  • \(e^{at}\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{1}{s-a}\)\(s>a\)
  • \(\sin(at)\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{a}{s^2+a^2}\)\(s>0\)
  • \(\cos(at)\)\(\mathcal{L}\)\(\longrightarrow\)\(\dfrac{s}{s^2+a^2}\)\(s>0\)
📐 3. Theorem: Linearity of the Laplace Transform
Theorem 1 (Linearity)

The Laplace transform is a linear operation. That is, for any functions \( f(t) \) and \( g(t) \) whose transforms exist, and any constants \( a \) and \( b \), the transform of \( af(t) + bg(t) \) exists, and

\[ \boxed{ \mathcal{L}\{af(t) + bg(t)\} = a\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\} + b\mathcal{L}\{g(t)\} } \]
📝 4. Proof of Linearity

Since integration is a linear operation:

\(\mathcal{L}\{af(t) + bg(t)\} = \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} \bigl[ af(t) + bg(t) \bigr] \, dt\)
\(= a \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} f(t) \, dt + b \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} g(t) \, dt\)
\(= a\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\} + b\mathcal{L}\{g(t)\}.\)

✅ This proves that the Laplace transform is linear.

📊 5. Application – Hyperbolic Functions

Find \(\mathcal{L}\{\cosh at\}\) and \(\mathcal{L}\{\sinh at\}\).

Using \(\cosh at = \frac{1}{2}(e^{at} + e^{-at})\) and \(\sinh at = \frac{1}{2}(e^{at} - e^{-at})\):

\(\mathcal{L}\{\cosh at\} = \dfrac{1}{2}\left( \mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\} + \mathcal{L}\{e^{-at}\} \right)\)
\(= \dfrac{1}{2}\left( \dfrac{1}{s-a} + \dfrac{1}{s+a} \right) = \dfrac{s}{s^2 - a^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}\{\sinh at\} = \dfrac{1}{2}\left( \mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\} - \mathcal{L}\{e^{-at}\} \right)\)
\(= \dfrac{1}{2}\left( \dfrac{1}{s-a} - \dfrac{1}{s+a} \right) = \dfrac{a}{s^2 - a^2}\)

✅ Thus we have derived the standard transforms for hyperbolic functions using linearity.

🔄 6. Linearity of the Inverse Laplace Transform
Theorem 2 (Inverse Linearity)

Since the Laplace transform is linear, its inverse is also linear. For any constants \( a \) and \( b \):

\[ \boxed{ \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{aF(s) + bG(s)\} = a\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} + b\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{G(s)\} } \]

This allows us to break complicated \( s \)-domain expressions into simpler parts and invert each term separately.

📚 7. Workout Examples – Inverse Laplace

Find \( f(t) = \mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} \) for each. Click Answer to see step-by-step solution.

25. \( \dfrac{0.2s + 1.8}{s^2 + 3.24} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(F(s) = \dfrac{0.2s + 1.8}{s^2 + 3.24}\)
\(= 0.2 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 + (1.8)^2} + 1.8 \cdot \dfrac{1}{s^2 + (1.8)^2}\)
\(= 0.2 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 + (1.8)^2} + \dfrac{1.8}{1.8} \cdot \dfrac{1.8}{s^2 + (1.8)^2}\)
\(= 0.2 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 + (1.8)^2} + 1 \cdot \dfrac{1.8}{s^2 + (1.8)^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = 0.2\cos(1.8t) + \sin(1.8t)\)

✅ \( f(t) = 0.2\cos(1.8t) + \sin(1.8t) \)

26. \( \dfrac{5s + 1}{s^2 - 25} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(F(s) = \dfrac{5s + 1}{s^2 - 25} = 5 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 - 5^2} + 1 \cdot \dfrac{1}{s^2 - 5^2}\)
\(= 5 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 - 5^2} + \dfrac{1}{5} \cdot \dfrac{5}{s^2 - 5^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = 5\cosh(5t) + \dfrac{1}{5}\sinh(5t)\)

✅ \( f(t) = 5\cosh(5t) + \dfrac{1}{5}\sinh(5t) \)

27. \( \dfrac{s}{L^2 s^2 + n^2 \pi^2} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(F(s) = \dfrac{s}{L^2 s^2 + n^2 \pi^2} = \dfrac{1}{L^2} \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 + \left(\dfrac{n\pi}{L}\right)^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = \dfrac{1}{L^2} \cos\left(\dfrac{n\pi}{L}t\right)\)

✅ \( f(t) = \dfrac{1}{L^2} \cos\left(\dfrac{n\pi}{L}t\right) \)

28. \( \dfrac{1}{(s + \sqrt{2})(s - \sqrt{3})} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step (Partial Fractions):
\(\dfrac{1}{(s + \sqrt{2})(s - \sqrt{3})} = \dfrac{A}{s + \sqrt{2}} + \dfrac{B}{s - \sqrt{3}}\)
\(1 = A(s - \sqrt{3}) + B(s + \sqrt{2})\)
\(A = -\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}, \quad B = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}\)
\(F(s) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}} \left( \dfrac{1}{s - \sqrt{3}} - \dfrac{1}{s + \sqrt{2}} \right)\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}} \left( e^{\sqrt{3}t} - e^{-\sqrt{2}t} \right)\)

✅ \( f(t) = \dfrac{e^{\sqrt{3}t} - e^{-\sqrt{2}t}}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}} \)

29. \( \dfrac{12}{s^4} - \dfrac{228}{s^6} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(F(s) = 12 \cdot \dfrac{1}{s^4} - 228 \cdot \dfrac{1}{s^6}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = 12 \cdot \dfrac{t^3}{3!} - 228 \cdot \dfrac{t^5}{5!}\)
\(= 12 \cdot \dfrac{t^3}{6} - 228 \cdot \dfrac{t^5}{120}\)
\(= 2t^3 - \dfrac{19}{10}t^5\)

✅ \( f(t) = 2t^3 - \dfrac{19}{10}t^5 \)

30. \( \dfrac{4s + 32}{s^2 - 16} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step:
\(F(s) = \dfrac{4s + 32}{s^2 - 16} = 4 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 - 4^2} + 32 \cdot \dfrac{1}{s^2 - 4^2}\)
\(= 4 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 - 4^2} + \dfrac{32}{4} \cdot \dfrac{4}{s^2 - 4^2}\)
\(= 4 \cdot \dfrac{s}{s^2 - 4^2} + 8 \cdot \dfrac{4}{s^2 - 4^2}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = 4\cosh(4t) + 8\sinh(4t)\)

✅ \( f(t) = 4\cosh(4t) + 8\sinh(4t) \)

31. \( \dfrac{s + 10}{s^2 - s - 2} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step (Factor + Partial Fractions):
\(s^2 - s - 2 = (s - 2)(s + 1)\)
\(\dfrac{s + 10}{(s - 2)(s + 1)} = \dfrac{A}{s - 2} + \dfrac{B}{s + 1}\)
\(s + 10 = A(s + 1) + B(s - 2)\)
\(s = 2: \quad 12 = 3A \Rightarrow A = 4\)
\(s = -1: \quad 9 = -3B \Rightarrow B = -3\)
\(F(s) = \dfrac{4}{s - 2} - \dfrac{3}{s + 1}\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = 4e^{2t} - 3e^{-t}\)

✅ \( f(t) = 4e^{2t} - 3e^{-t} \)

32. \( \dfrac{1}{(s + a)(s + b)} \) CO2
✅ Step-by-step (Partial Fractions):
\(\dfrac{1}{(s + a)(s + b)} = \dfrac{A}{s + a} + \dfrac{B}{s + b}\)
\(1 = A(s + b) + B(s + a)\)
\(s = -a: \quad 1 = A(b - a) \Rightarrow A = \dfrac{1}{b - a}\)
\(s = -b: \quad 1 = B(a - b) \Rightarrow B = \dfrac{1}{a - b} = -\dfrac{1}{b - a}\)
\(F(s) = \dfrac{1}{b - a} \left( \dfrac{1}{s + a} - \dfrac{1}{s + b} \right)\)
\(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\} = \dfrac{1}{b - a} \left( e^{-at} - e^{-bt} \right)\)

✅ \( f(t) = \dfrac{e^{-at} - e^{-bt}}{b - a} \)

📋 8. Quick Summary
  • Linearity (Laplace): \(\mathcal{L}\{af + bg\} = a\mathcal{L}\{f\} + b\mathcal{L}\{g\}\)
  • Linearity (Inverse): \(\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{aF + bG\} = a\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F\} + b\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{G\}\)
  • Hyperbolic: \(\mathcal{L}\{\cosh at\} = \dfrac{s}{s^2-a^2}\), \(\mathcal{L}\{\sinh at\} = \dfrac{a}{s^2-a^2}\)
