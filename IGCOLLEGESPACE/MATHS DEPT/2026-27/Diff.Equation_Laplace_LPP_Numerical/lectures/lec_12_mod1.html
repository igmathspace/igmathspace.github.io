📖 Bernoulli Equation – Step-by-Step Method
📌 Bernoulli Equation

A Bernoulli equation has the form:

\[ y' + p(x)\,y = g(x)\,y^a \]

where \(a\) is any real number. If \(a = 0\) or \(a = 1\), the equation is linear. Otherwise, it is nonlinear.

⚡ Step-by-Step Solution Method
📐 Standard Procedure for Bernoulli Equations
Step 1: Identify the exponent \(a\) in \( y' + p(x)y = g(x)y^a \).
Step 2: Divide throughout by \(y^a\): \[ y^{-a}y' + p(x)y^{1-a} = g(x) \]
Step 3: Substitute \( z = y^{1-a} \). Then \( z' = (1-a)y^{-a}y' \), so: \[ \frac{1}{1-a} z' + p(x) z = g(x) \quad \Rightarrow \quad z' + (1-a)p(x)z = (1-a)g(x) \]
Step 4: Solve the resulting linear ODE in \(z\) using the integrating factor method.
Step 5: Substitute back \( z = y^{1-a} \) to get the solution in terms of \(y\).
💡 Special cases: \( y = 0 \) is often a solution (especially when \(a > 0\)). Don't forget to include it if applicable.
📖 Worked Example

Problem: \( y' + y = y^2 \)

Step 1: \( a = 2 \), \( p(x) = 1 \), \( g(x) = 1 \).
Step 2: Divide by \( y^2 \): \( y^{-2}y' + y^{-1} = 1 \).
Step 3: Let \( z = y^{1-2} = y^{-1} \). Then \( z' = -y^{-2}y' \). So \( -z' + z = 1 \) → \( z' - z = -1 \).
Step 4: Linear in \(z\): \( z' - z = -1 \), IF = \( e^{-x} \). \( (z e^{-x})' = -e^{-x} \) → \( z e^{-x} = e^{-x} + C \) → \( z = 1 + C e^{x} \).
Step 5: \( \frac{1}{y} = 1 + C e^{x} \) → \( y = \frac{1}{1 + C e^{x}} \). Also \( y = 0 \) is a solution.
📝 Practice Problems
22. \( y' + y = y^2, \quad y(0) = -\frac{1}{3} \)
Step 1: \( a = 2 \), \( p(x)=1 \), \( g(x)=1 \).
Step 2: Divide by \( y^2 \): \( y^{-2}y' + y^{-1} = 1 \).
Step 3: \( z = y^{-1} \), \( z' = -y^{-2}y' \) → \( -z' + z = 1 \) → \( z' - z = -1 \).
Step 4: IF = \( e^{-x} \). \( (z e^{-x})' = -e^{-x} \) → \( z = 1 + C e^{x} \).
Step 5: \( y = \frac{1}{1 + C e^{x}} \). IC: \( -\frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{1+C} \) → \( C = -4 \).
Solution: \( y = \frac{1}{1 - 4 e^{x}} \).
23. \( y' + xy = xy^{-1}, \quad y(0) = 3 \)
Step 1: \( a = -1 \), \( p(x)=x \), \( g(x)=x \).
Step 2: Divide by \( y^{-1} \): \( y^{1}y' + x y^{2} = x \) → \( y y' + x y^2 = x \).
Step 3: \( z = y^{1-(-1)} = y^2 \), \( z' = 2y y' \) → \( \frac{1}{2}z' + x z = x \) → \( z' + 2x z = 2x \).
Step 4: IF = \( e^{x^2} \). \( (z e^{x^2})' = 2x e^{x^2} \) → \( z = 1 + C e^{-x^2} \).
Step 5: \( y^2 = 1 + C e^{-x^2} \). IC: \( 9 = 1 + C \) → \( C = 8 \).
Solution: \( y = \sqrt{1 + 8 e^{-x^2}} \) (positive branch).
24. \( y' + y = -x/y \)
Step 1: Multiply by \( y \): \( y y' + y^2 = -x \). Here \( a = -1 \) if written as \( y' + y = -x y^{-1} \).
Step 2: Divide by \( y^{-1} \): \( y y' + y^2 = -x \).
Step 3: \( z = y^2 \), \( z' = 2y y' \) → \( \frac{1}{2}z' + z = -x \) → \( z' + 2z = -2x \).
Step 4: IF = \( e^{2x} \). \( (z e^{2x})' = -2x e^{2x} \) → \( z e^{2x} = -x e^{2x} + \frac{1}{2} e^{2x} + C \).
Step 5: \( z = -x + \frac{1}{2} + C e^{-2x} \) → \( y^2 = -x + \frac{1}{2} + C e^{-2x} \).
25. \( y' = 3.2y - 10y^2 \)
Step 1: \( y' - 3.2y = -10y^2 \) → \( a = 2 \), \( p(x) = -3.2 \), \( g(x) = -10 \).
Step 2: Divide by \( y^2 \): \( y^{-2}y' - 3.2 y^{-1} = -10 \).
Step 3: \( z = y^{-1} \), \( z' = -y^{-2}y' \) → \( -z' - 3.2 z = -10 \) → \( z' + 3.2 z = 10 \).
Step 4: IF = \( e^{3.2x} \). \( (z e^{3.2x})' = 10 e^{3.2x} \) → \( z = 3.125 + C e^{-3.2x} \).
Step 5: \( y = \frac{1}{3.125 + C e^{-3.2x}} \). Also \( y = 0 \) is a solution.
26. \( y' = \frac{\tan y}{x - 1}, \quad y(0) = \frac{\pi}{2} \)
Step 1: This is not in Bernoulli form directly. Rewrite: \( \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\tan y}{x-1} \).
Step 2: Invert: \( \frac{dx}{dy} = \frac{x-1}{\tan y} \) → \( \frac{dx}{dy} - (\cot y) x = -\cot y \).
Step 3: Linear in \( x(y) \): \( p(y) = -\cot y \), \( q(y) = -\cot y \). IF = \( e^{\int -\cot y \, dy} = \csc y \).
Step 4: \( (x \csc y)' = -\cot y \csc y \) → \( x \csc y = \csc y + C \).
Step 5: \( x = 1 + C \sin y \). IC: \( y(0)=\pi/2 \) → \( 0 = 1 + C \) → \( C = -1 \).
Solution: \( x = 1 - \sin y \) (implicit).
📋 Quick Summary
  • Bernoulli form: \( y' + p(x)y = g(x)y^a \)
  • Divide by \( y^a \): \( y^{-a}y' + p(x)y^{1-a} = g(x) \)
  • Substitute: \( z = y^{1-a} \) → \( z' + (1-a)p(x)z = (1-a)g(x) \)
  • Solve linear ODE for \( z \), then substitute back.
  • Logistic equation: \( y' = Ay - By^2 \) → \( u' + A u = B \)
