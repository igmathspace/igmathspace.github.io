📖 Linear ODEs – Shortcut Method (Homogeneous & Nonhomogeneous)
📌 Definition: Linear ODE

A first-order linear ordinary differential equation can be written in the standard form:

\[ y' + p(x)\,y = q(x) \]

where \(p(x)\) and \(q(x)\) are functions of \(x\) (or constants).

  • Homogeneous if \(q(x) = 0\) → \(y' + p(x)y = 0\)
  • Nonhomogeneous if \(q(x) \neq 0\)
⚡ Shortcut Method (Integrating Factor)
📐 Step-by-Step Shortcut
Step 1: Rewrite the equation in the form \( y' + p(x)y = q(x) \) and identify \( p(x) \) and \( q(x) \) with their signs.
Step 2: Find the integrating factor:
\[ \text{IF} = e^{\int p(x) \, dx} \]
Step 3: Finalize the solution using the ready-made frame:
\[ y \times \text{IF} = \int \big( q(x) \times \text{IF} \big) \, dx \]

Then solve for \(y\) and add the constant of integration \(C\).

💡 Note: If the independent variable is \(t\), replace \(x\) with \(t\): \[ y \times \text{IF} = \int \big( q(t) \times \text{IF} \big) \, dt, \quad \text{IF} = e^{\int p(t) dt} \]
📝 Problems (with Shortcut Solutions)
1. (Homogeneous) \( y' + 3y = 0 \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = 3 \), \( q(x) = 0 \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{\int 3 dx} = e^{3x} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{3x} = \int 0 \, dx = C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = C e^{-3x} \)
2. (Homogeneous) \( y' - 2xy = 0 \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = -2x \), \( q(x) = 0 \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{\int -2x dx} = e^{-x^2} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{-x^2} = \int 0 \, dx = C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = C e^{x^2} \)
3. \( y' - y = 5.2 \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = -1 \), \( q(x) = 5.2 \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{-x} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{-x} = \int 5.2 e^{-x} dx = -5.2 e^{-x} + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = -5.2 + C e^{x} \)
4. \( y' = 2y - 4x \)
Step 1: \( y' - 2y = -4x \) → \( p(x) = -2 \), \( q(x) = -4x \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{-2x} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{-2x} = \int (-4x) e^{-2x} dx = 2x e^{-2x} + e^{-2x} + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = 2x + 1 + C e^{2x} \)
5. \( y' + ky = e^{-kx} \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = k \), \( q(x) = e^{-kx} \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{kx} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{kx} = \int 1 \, dx = x + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = (x + C) e^{-kx} \)
6. \( y' + 2y = 4 \cos 2x, \quad y(\pi/4) = 3 \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = 2 \), \( q(x) = 4 \cos 2x \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{2x} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{2x} = \int 4 \cos 2x \, e^{2x} dx = e^{2x}(\cos 2x + \sin 2x) + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = \cos 2x + \sin 2x + C e^{-2x} \)
IC: \( C = 2 e^{\pi/2} \) → \( y = \cos 2x + \sin 2x + 2 e^{\pi/2 - 2x} \)
7. \( x y' = 2y + x^3 e^x \)
Step 1: \( y' - \frac{2}{x}y = x^2 e^x \) → \( p(x) = -\frac{2}{x} \), \( q(x) = x^2 e^x \)
Step 2: IF = \( x^{-2} \)
Step 3: \( y x^{-2} = \int e^x dx = e^x + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = x^2 (e^x + C) \)
8. \( y' + y \tan x = e^{-0.01x} \cos x, \quad y(0) = 0 \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = \tan x \), \( q(x) = e^{-0.01x} \cos x \)
Step 2: IF = \( \sec x \)
Step 3: \( y \sec x = \int e^{-0.01x} dx = -100 e^{-0.01x} + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = \cos x (-100 e^{-0.01x} + C) \)
IC: \( C = 100 \) → \( y = 100 \cos x (1 - e^{-0.01x}) \)
9. \( y' + y \sin x = e^{\cos x}, \quad y(0) = -2.5 \)
Step 1: \( p(x) = \sin x \), \( q(x) = e^{\cos x} \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{-\cos x} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{-\cos x} = \int 1 \, dx = x + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = e^{\cos x} (x + C) \)
IC: \( C = -2.5 e^{-1} \) → \( y = e^{\cos x} (x - 2.5 e^{-1}) \)
10. \( y' \cos x + (3y - 1) \sec x = 0, \quad y(\pi/4) = 4/3 \)
Step 1: \( y' + 3\sec^2 x \, y = \sec^2 x \) → \( p(x) = 3\sec^2 x \), \( q(x) = \sec^2 x \)
Step 2: IF = \( e^{3\tan x} \)
Step 3: \( y e^{3\tan x} = \frac{1}{3} e^{3\tan x} + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = \frac{1}{3} + C e^{-3\tan x} \)
IC: \( C = e^{3} \) → \( y = \frac{1}{3} + e^{3(1-\tan x)} \)
11. \( y' = (y - 2) \cot x \)
Step 1: \( y' - (\cot x) y = -2\cot x \) → \( p(x) = -\cot x \), \( q(x) = -2\cot x \)
Step 2: IF = \( \csc x \)
Step 3: \( y \csc x = \int (-2\cot x) \csc x \, dx = 2 \csc x + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = 2 + C \sin x \)
12. \( x y' + 4y = 8x^4, \quad y(1) = 2 \)
Step 1: \( y' + \frac{4}{x}y = 8x^3 \) → \( p(x) = \frac{4}{x} \), \( q(x) = 8x^3 \)
Step 2: IF = \( x^4 \)
Step 3: \( y x^4 = \int 8x^7 dx = x^8 + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = x^4 + C x^{-4} \)
IC: \( C = 1 \) → \( y = x^4 + x^{-4} \)
13. \( y' = 6(y - 2.5) \tanh(1.5x) \)
Step 1: \( y' - 6 \tanh(1.5x) \, y = -15 \tanh(1.5x) \)
\( p(x) = -6 \tanh(1.5x) \), \( q(x) = -15 \tanh(1.5x) \)
Step 2: IF = \( \cosh^{-4}(1.5x) \)
Step 3: \( y \cosh^{-4}(1.5x) = \int (-15 \tanh(1.5x)) \cosh^{-4}(1.5x) dx = \frac{5}{2} \cosh^{-4}(1.5x) + C \)
\( \Rightarrow y = \frac{5}{2} + C \cosh^4(1.5x) \)
📋 Quick Summary
  • Standard form: \( y' + p(x)y = q(x) \)
  • Integrating factor: \( \text{IF} = e^{\int p(x) dx} \)
  • Solution frame: \( y \cdot \text{IF} = \int (q \cdot \text{IF}) \, dx + C \)
  • Homogeneous: \( q(x) = 0 \) → solution: \( y = C e^{-\int p(x) dx} \)
  • Nonhomogeneous: use the shortcut frame above.
