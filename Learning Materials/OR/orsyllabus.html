Course Description

This course covers the fundamentals of Operations Research including the historical background, mathematical formulation of LPP, graphical solution method, simplex method, transportation Problem, assignment problem and sequencing problems.

Course Prerequisite

Basic understanding of linear algebra, calculus and probability.
